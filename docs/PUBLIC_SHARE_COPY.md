## 中文｜约 300 字

**我们能否学会更好地竞争与合作？**

竞争不会消失，合作也不是天然正确。真正值得研究的是：当每个主体都在维护自身利益时，能否更早看见合作与不合作分别会带来什么收益、损害、风险与长期后果？能否让没有坐在谈判桌前却承担代价的人也进入判断？能否让信任不再只是合作的前提，而是在小规模、可退出、可核验的履约中逐渐形成？

“竞争—合作可计算机制研究”尝试把这些问题变成可以检查、复算和反驳的对象。这里的“可计算”不是给人打分，也不是让算法决定什么是公平，而是把主体、结果、约束、未知和失败条件写清楚。

项目已经开放源代码与公开测试。你可以带来企业、供应链、劳资、平台、社区或其他多主体问题；也可以直接复现、击破或独立实现。

## English | ~150 words

**Can we learn to compete and cooperate better?**

Competition will not disappear, and cooperation is not automatically good. The more useful question is whether actors protecting their own interests can see earlier what cooperation and non-cooperation each gain, cost, and risk—and who bears consequences without being at the table.

Computable Competition–Cooperation Mechanisms turns part of that question into something inspectable, recomputable, and falsifiable. “Computable” does not mean scoring people or letting an algorithm decide fairness. It means making actors, declared outcomes, constraints, unknowns, and failure conditions explicit enough to challenge.

The project is open source and public testing is open. Bring a merger, supply-chain, labor, platform, community, or other multi-actor problem—or simply reproduce, break, or reimplement the reference checker.

Idea guide: <https://github.com/Civilization-Leap/computable-cooperation-mechanisms/blob/main/docs/PUBLIC_IDEA_GUIDE_EN.md>

Public test: <https://github.com/Civilization-Leap/computable-cooperation-mechanisms/issues/21>

## English | short social copy

What does cooperation gain and cost? What does non-cooperation gain and cost? Who benefits, who bears the burden, and who was never at the table? Computable Competition–Cooperation Mechanisms is an open-source attempt to make those conditions more explicit, recomputable, and challengeable—without pretending computation can confer legitimacy. Public testing: <https://github.com/Civilization-Leap/computable-cooperation-mechanisms/issues/21>
