## 中文｜通用长帖（知乎 / 公众号 / 国内专业社群）

### 我们能否学会更好地竞争与合作？

竞争不会消失，合作也不是天然正确。

真正值得研究的，也许不是“怎样让所有人合作”，而是：当不同主体都在维护自身利益时，能否更早看见合作与不合作分别会带来什么收益、损害、风险与长期后果？谁获益，谁承担，谁甚至没有坐在谈判桌前？

很多冲突并不是从“利益绝对不可调和”开始，而是从立场遮蔽真实利益、风险被外部化、第三方没有发言权、承诺无法核验，以及对彼此未来行为的错误预期开始。

“可计算”不是让算法决定什么是公平，也不是给人打分。它首先是一种反含混纪律：把主体、结果、约束、第三方影响、未知、退出条件和失败条件尽量写清楚，让别人能够检查、复算、质疑、反驳和改进。

精确计算可以执行规则，但不能赋予规则正当性。

这个项目正在把其中一部分机制做成开放、可运行、可复算、可独立重实现的最小参考系统。它不要求你认同框架。欢迎带来真实问题、反例、失败案例，或者直接证明它哪里不成立。

## English | LinkedIn / long social post

### Can we learn to compete and cooperate better?

Competition will not disappear. Cooperation is not automatically good.

A more useful question may be this: when different actors are protecting their own interests, can they see earlier what cooperation and non-cooperation each gain, cost, and risk — and who bears consequences without being at the table?

Many conflicts do not begin with absolutely incompatible interests. They begin with positions hiding underlying interests, costs shifted to third parties, unverifiable commitments, missing exit conditions, and mistaken expectations about what others will do next.

“Computable” here does not mean letting an algorithm decide fairness. It means making actors, declared outcomes, constraints, third-party effects, unknowns, exit conditions, and failure conditions explicit enough to inspect, recompute, challenge, falsify, and improve.

Precise computation can enforce a rule. It cannot confer legitimacy on the rule.

We are turning a small part of this idea into an open, runnable, reproducible reference implementation. Endorsement is not requested. Counterexamples, failure cases, independent reimplementations, and real multi-actor problems are welcome.

## Evidence discipline

Record each channel as one of:

- READY — copy prepared, not sent.
- SCHEDULED — accepted by a scheduler, not yet published.
- PUBLISHED — publicly visible URL exists.
- REDISTRIBUTED — third party independently shared it.
- ENGAGED — substantive comment, issue, test, or response exists.
- VERIFIED — only for a separately evidenced reproduction or audit; never infer from engagement.
