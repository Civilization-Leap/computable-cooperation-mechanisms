# Public Outreach Wave 3

Date: 2026-09-13

Purpose: broaden public awareness of the project idea itself — a more explicit, inspectable, and challengeable way to reason about competition and cooperation — rather than treating the software test as the primary entry point.

## Public framing used

Core question:

> When different actors are all protecting their own interests, can we make visible earlier what cooperation and non-cooperation each gain, cost, and risk — and who bears consequences without being at the table?

Core boundary:

> Precise computation can enforce a rule. It cannot confer legitimacy on the rule.

## Evidence discipline

`CONTACTED` means an outbound message was accepted by Gmail and marked `SENT`. It does **not** mean the message was delivered, read, forwarded, accepted, endorsed, or acted upon.

Any later state must be recorded separately, for example:

`CONTACTED -> REPLIED -> SHARED / PUBLISHED / TESTED / CRITIQUED / NO_FURTHER_ACTION`

Silence is not treated as rejection or validation.

## Why these channels

Wave 3 intentionally targets communities where the project purpose can stand on its own before the reader sees code:

- collective intelligence and democratic AI;
- digital governance and institutional experimentation;
- open knowledge and public-interest technology.

This wave does not replace developer/testing outreach. It adds a broader upstream entry: understand the problem first, then choose whether to discuss, use, test, criticize, fork, or ignore the implementation.
