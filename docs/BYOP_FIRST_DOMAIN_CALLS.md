# BYOP First Domain Problem Calls

The project is now asking for **real problems**, not endorsements.

The first intake wave focuses on domains where competition/cooperation choices are common, consequences are material, and synthetic/anonymized cases can usually be shared without exposing confidential details.

## 1. Commercial negotiation and supply relationships

Examples:

- supplier dependence;
- price vs. delivery certainty;
- acquisition vs. long-term contracting;
- minority investment vs. control;
- joint capacity investment;
- exit rights and fallback suppliers.

Useful question:

> What do cooperation and non-cooperation each gain and cost, and which actor or third party carries the downside?

## 2. Labor and organizational arrangements

Examples:

- wage vs. staffing stability;
- automation vs. redeployment;
- productivity targets vs. safety or workload limits;
- restructuring vs. retention;
- short-term savings vs. long-term skill loss.

Useful question:

> Which conditions are ordinary trade-offs, and which should be represented as non-offsettable hard boundaries?

## 3. Platform and ecosystem governance

Examples:

- fee changes;
- ranking or recommendation rules;
- creator / merchant / platform revenue allocation;
- moderation or access rules;
- growth vs. ecosystem stability.

Useful question:

> Does a platform-side gain shift risk or cost onto participants who did not define the rule?

## 4. Public governance and civic technology

Examples:

- resource allocation;
- public-service redesign;
- infrastructure siting;
- participation rules;
- policy choices with concentrated benefits and diffuse costs.

Useful question:

> Who benefits, who bears the burden, and who is absent from the decision process?

## 5. AI governance and high-consequence technology

Public BYOP submissions in this domain should remain at the level of governance structure, deployment choice, evidence, constraints, accountability, reversibility, and third-party impact.

Do **not** submit operational instructions for weapons, harmful capabilities, intrusion, or evasion.

Useful question:

> Which benefits, risks, unknowns, and irreversible effects need to be visible before a deployment or competitive escalation becomes difficult to reverse?

## What to send

Open the **Bring Your Own Problem** issue form and provide the smallest synthetic/anonymized case that preserves the mechanism under challenge.

A high-value submission may show that the current tool works, but it may also show:

- a representation gap;
- a missing actor or constraint;
- a counterexample;
- a likely misinterpretation;
- a recurring domain need that should become a reusable template.

## What not to send

Do not post:

- confidential contracts;
- personal data;
- proprietary datasets;
- private bargaining positions;
- credentials;
- classified or security-sensitive operational information.

The goal is to expose the **mechanism**, not the secret details of a live dispute.
