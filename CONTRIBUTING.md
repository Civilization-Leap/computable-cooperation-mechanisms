# Contributing

Keep changes small, explicit, and testable. Do not add a universal fairness score, silently coerce UNKNOWN to zero, or make ordinary gains override a hard constraint. New semantics require tests and documentation. Do not submit restricted research fixtures, personal data, credentials, or real-world authorization material.
