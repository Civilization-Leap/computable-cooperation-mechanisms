# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they keep the project small, inspectable, testable, and explicit about scope.

## Good contributions

Useful contributions include bug fixes, stricter validation, reproducibility improvements, counterexamples, failure cases, new original synthetic examples, clearer documentation, translations, and well-scoped mechanism extensions.

## Required discipline

- Do not add a universal fairness, welfare, cooperation, loyalty, ideology, personality, or civilization score.
- Do not silently coerce `UNKNOWN` to zero, false, success, or failure.
- Do not make ordinary gains automatically override a hard constraint.
- Do not convert invalid references or undeclared units into `UNKNOWN`.
- New semantics require explicit code, tests, and documentation.
- Keep domain-specific assumptions visibly domain-specific; do not present one scenario as a universal institution.
- Do not submit controlled research fixtures, restricted materials, personal data, credentials, secrets, or real-world authorization material.
- Software test success must not be described as fairness certification, formal research validation, or real-world authorization.
